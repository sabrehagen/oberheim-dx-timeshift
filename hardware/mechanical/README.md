# hardware/mechanical: drill template, wiring diagram, cable mapping

DX TimeShift rev A, 2026-11-06. Every drawing here is **generated by a script**. To change one,
edit the inputs and regenerate. Don't hand-edit the SVG, PDF or DXF.

## Regenerate

```bash
pip install --user ezdxf cairosvg          # or: sudo apt-get install -y librsvg2-bin  (PDF/PNG via rsvg-convert)
python3 hardware/mechanical/tools/build_all.py
```

On Debian/Ubuntu Python 3.12+ you may need `pip install --user --break-system-packages ezdxf cairosvg`,
or use a virtualenv. With no ezdxf the DXF is skipped. With neither cairosvg nor
rsvg-convert the script stops and tells you what to install.

One-off overrides (these are not saved; editing the JSON is better):

```bash
python3 hardware/mechanical/tools/build_all.py --set pitch_hihat_snare_mm=20.8 --set lug_direction=left
```

The script prints the automatic checks. The template's STATUS line says:

* **"ESTIMATES ONLY — DO NOT DRILL"** while any required value still has `"measured": false`
  (this is how the repo ships: the defaults were scaled from a 520-pixel photo and are only good to about ±3 mm),
* **"MEASURED, BUT A CHECK FAILED — DO NOT DRILL"** if a clearance check says STOP,
* **"generated from measured values"** when everything is measured and passes.

## Measurement checklist (do this before printing the template for real)

Mains off and unplugged. Lid open (2 front screws). Digital calipers and a small steel rule.
"Outside" means looking at the rear of the DX: the BASS pot is on your right. Write each value
into `tools/panel_measurements.json` and set `"measured": true`.

| # | JSON key | Measure | Default (photo estimate) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `pitch_hihat_snare_mm` | Outside: HI-HAT shaft centre to SNARE shaft centre. Measure left edge to left edge of the two shafts. | 20.5 |
| 2 | `pitch_snare_bass_mm` | Outside: SNARE shaft centre to BASS shaft centre. | 20.5 |
| 3 | `shaft_dia_mm` | Diameter of the existing tuning shafts. Note too whether they are knurled or slotted, and how far they stick out. | 6.0 |
| 4 | `screw_right_of_snare_mm`, `screw_below_snare_mm` | Outside: the screw head just below and to the right of the SNARE pot (it holds the tuning-pot PCB). Horizontal and vertical distance from the SNARE shaft centre to the screw centre. | 6.3, 9.8 |
| 5 | `screw_head_dia_mm` | That screw head's diameter. | 5.5 |
| 6 | `outputs_label_top_below_snare_mm` | Outside: SNARE shaft centre down to the top of the printed "SNARE" output-jack label text. | 29.9 |
| 7 | `panel_thickness_mm` | Sheet thickness at the edge of a jack hole. Must be ≤ 3.6 mm for the pot's M7 bushing. | 1.5 |
| 8 | `tuning_pcb_bottom_below_snare_mm` | **Behind the panel** (findings Q6): from the SNARE shaft centre line down to the lowest edge of the tuning-pot PCB, its standoffs or any wire, within 15 mm either side. | not measured |
| 9 | `jack_body_top_below_snare_mm` | **Behind the panel**: from the SNARE shaft centre line down to the top of the nearest board-mounted jack body (or anything else rising from the main board) near the planned hole. | not measured |
| 10 | `free_depth_behind_panel_mm` | **Behind the panel**, at the planned hole: free depth from the inside face of the panel to the first obstruction. Needs ≥ 25 mm. | not measured |
| 11 | `new_hole_below_snare_mm` | **Your decision** after 4–10: the centre of the new hole. Put it midway between the screw head and the OUTPUTS label, AND so that the 17 mm pot body fits between items 8 and 9 with ≥ 1 mm to spare each side. | 21.0 |
| 12 | `new_hole_right_of_snare_mm` | Keep 0 (directly below SNARE) unless something behind the panel is in the way. | 0.0 |
| 13 | `lug_direction` | Which way the pot's solder lugs point, seen from outside. `right` or `left` keeps the pot's height to its 17 mm body. The checks tell you if the tab hole would hit the screw or the label. | right |
| 14 | `drill_tab_hole` | `false` = snap the anti-rotation tab off the pot (clean look; the nut + washer hold it). `true` = drill the Ø3.0 mm tab hole too. | false |

After printing, the template is self-checking: the three shaft cut-outs **and** the screw circle
**and** the label line must all line up on the real panel. If one doesn't, a measurement is wrong.

Also record (these feed the other docs, not the template): the U701 marking and whether it is
socketed (Q1), the lid clearance above the planned board location (Q3, modelling-clay method; the
84 × 63 mm board with the Nano needs a footprint that size and about 32 mm of height on 12.7 mm supports), and whether a DX SYNC-TO-TAPE 1760B or
Electrongate MIDI board sits in ZONE A.

## Design notes

* **Pot: TT Electronics/BI P160KNP-0EC15B10K.** 16 mm, 10 k linear, solder lugs, a 6 mm slotted
  shaft 15 mm long, M7×0.75 bushing, nut and washer supplied. The slotted bare shaft matches the
  DX's knob-less tuning pots. **Panel hole Ø7.5–8.0 mm**: the datasheet asks for 7.5 +0.2/−0, and
  8.0 mm (the usual step-drill step) is also acceptable because the M7 nut (9.9 mm A/F) and the
  Ø12 mm washer cover and clamp it. Tab hole Ø3.0 mm at 7.8 mm (datasheet p10).
  The knurled version (P160KNP-0QC15B10K) plus a small knob is the alternative (see the BOM). If the
  behind-panel band is too small for a 17 mm body, change the `pot` block in the JSON (hole Ø, body
  Ø, depth, tab) for a 9 mm-class pot and regenerate.
* **Lug numbering.** Seen from the shaft side with the lugs down, the lugs are 1-2-3 left to right;
  from inside the DX they are mirrored. Don't trust the picture: with the shaft fully anticlockwise
  (seen from outside), lug 1 ↔ lug 2 reads ≈ 0 Ω. Lug 1 → J2-1 GND, lug 2 → J2-2 wiper,
  lug 3 → J2-3 +5 V. Anticlockwise = 0 (bypass), clockwise = 1 beat.
* **Clock tap.** Mini-grabber (Pomona 3925) on the R791 lead at the **Q709-collector** side. With the
  DX on and stopped it reads ≈ 0 V (the other lead reads 5 V). With the DX off it has 0 Ω to the CLOCK OUT tip.
  The **ground wire (J3 pin 2) is optional**. Connect it to a DX ground point that reads 0 Ω to U701
  pin 8, or leave it unconnected with its end insulated (heat-shrink); the ribbon already carries
  ground. Do not clip it to a Q709 leg: **which Q709 leg is the emitter is unconfirmed**.
* **PPQN jumper.** JP1 is a 2×2 header: no shunt = 96 PPQN (plain DX), shunt on **JP1 1-2** = 48
  (Nano D10), shunt on **JP1 3-4** = 24 (Nano D11). (J1/J2/J3 are connectors, not jumpers.)
* **Board (from the final KiCad layout).** 84.0 × 63.0 mm, 2 mm corner radius, 4 × M3 holes
  (Ø3.2 mm, non-plated) 3.5 mm in from each corner, so the hole centres are 77.0 × 56.0 mm apart.
  J1 (DX PLUG) is at the top centre, J2 POT and J3 CLK are on the right edge, and the Nano's USB end
  faces the left edge. These are the `BOARD_W`/`BOARD_H` constants in `tools/gen_wiring.py`.
* **Board location (suggestion, TBC).** ZONE A, directly in front of U701–U704, on four
  self-adhesive snap-lock supports, J1 facing U701, ribbon ≤ 15 cm. ZONE B (the blank area at
  the front right near R791) needs a 20–25 cm ribbon, so use it only after the GEN0* scope
  check (findings Q4). DX board positions on the diagram are scaled from the Oberheim parts
  layout drawing (±3 mm).
* **The J1↔DIP mapping** shown on both sheets comes from `hardware/kicad/tools/netlist.py`
  (`HDR2DIP`). If that file is missing, the brief §3.5 table built into `gen_wiring.py` is used,
  and the sheets say which source they used.
